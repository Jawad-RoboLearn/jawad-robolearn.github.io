About Me

I am a PhD researcher at the Embodied AI and Neurorobotics Lab, SDU Biorobotics, Mærsk Mc-Kinney Møller Institute, University of Southern Denmark, under the supervision of Prof. Poramate Manoonpong. I am a Marie Skłodowska-Curie (MSCA) doctoral candidate in the MAESTRI Doctoral Network.

My PhD focuses on magnetoactive soft robots capable of versatile locomotion. Before SDU, I completed an MPhil in Mechanical and Automation Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, where I worked on geometric control of morphable quadrotors, and an MS in Control Engineering at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, working on soft grippers and haptic sensing. I have also worked as a control engineer at ASMPT, Hong Kong.

I am currently looking for postdoctoral positions starting in late 2027 — feel free to get in touch.

Research Interests

I design and control soft robots driven by external magnetic fields — from multistable structures that enable multiple gaits with a single magnet, to proprioceptive sensing and closed-loop control of magnetic joints. More broadly, I am interested in how morphology and control work together to produce adaptive robot behaviour.
